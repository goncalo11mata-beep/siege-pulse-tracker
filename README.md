# Siege Success Strategy

Here is the complete conversion rate optimization (CRO) and messaging breakdown of the Ubisoft Rainbow Six Siege landing page (BR Portuguese version).

As a live-service gaming product, this page operates differently than a traditional B2B or SaaS landing page. It is highly visual, low on long-form text, and strictly optimized for immediate friction reduction (Free Access) and active player retention (FOMO via Seasons).

1. Full Content & Section Mapping

Section 1: The Hero / Primary Navigation

Raw Text/Copy: "A nova era de Rainbow Six Siege acaba de começar. / Acesso Gratuito / Comprar / Membership / Passe de Batalha / Itens no jogo / Créditos R6"

Functional Goal: Immediate segmenting. The page instantly divides traffic into two buckets: new players (Free Access/Buy) and existing players looking to spend money on microtransactions (Membership/Battle Pass/Credits).

Primary Emotional Driver: Excitement and FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out). The phrase "A nova era" (The new era) signals to churned players that the game has fundamentally improved, and to new players that this is the perfect starting point.

Section 2: The Offer Stack (Current Season Highlight)

Raw Text/Copy: "Destaques: PASSE DE BATALHA DA OPERATION SPLIT FIRE – DE 1º DE SETEMBRO A 1º DE DEZEMBRO. Complete partidas multiplayer para ganhar fichas do Passe de Batalha e desbloquear blocos. Obtenha o Passe Premium ou a Assinatura para ganhar ainda mais recompensas! Saiba mais."

Functional Goal: Establish extreme time-bound urgency and pitch the immediate upsell (Premium Pass/Subscription). It gives the user a specific, actionable reason to play right now.



Primary Emotional Driver: Achievement, Greed/Reward-seeking, and Urgency.



Section 3: Community & Social Proof (News Feed)



Raw Text/Copy: "ÚLTIMAS NOTÍCIAS... Novo Drop e distintivo da Twitch disponíveis durante o Evento Wasteland Circuit! / Y11S3 PATCH NOTES ADDENDUM / Novo Twitch Drop para a Operation Split Fire!"



Functional Goal: Act as dynamic social proof. Instead of traditional testimonials, gaming pages use active community events (Twitch drops, patch notes) to prove the game is highly populated, actively developed, and culturally relevant.

Primary Emotional Driver: Belonging, Connection, and Relevance.

Section 4: Feature Expansion & Product Update

Raw Text/Copy: "OPERATION SPLIT FIRE. A Operation Split Fire traz grandes atualizações de conteúdo, incluindo um novo agente neutralizador de escudo: Noor. Ele traz seu gadget Lança Hórus, projétil que adere a escudos balísticos ou superfícies e emite chamas! Esta temporada também introduz a nova Divisão Lendária para Por Colocação, um novo modo Arcade 3v3... Prepare-se: a nova temporada será lançada em 1.º de setembro! / Ver Notas da Atualização."

Functional Goal: Provide tangible proof of the "new era" claim from the Hero section. It introduces new mechanics to re-engage churned players and showcases the depth of the game to new prospects.

Primary Emotional Driver: Curiosity and Competitiveness.

2. Copywriting Patterns & Formula

Headline Mechanics:

Formula: The "Declarative Event" Statement. There are no questions or problem-agitation headlines (e.g., "Tired of boring shooters?"). Instead, it uses bold, factual declarations: "A nova era de Rainbow Six Siege acaba de começar" (The new era of Rainbow Six Siege has just begun).

Why it works: It commands authority. It frames the product not as a static software purchase, but as an ongoing, living event.

Primary Hooks & Angle:

UVP (Unique Value Proposition): Evolving tactical depth.

Narrative Angle: The page leans entirely into the "Live Service" angle. The hook isn't just about buying a game; it's about joining an ever-changing ecosystem right at the start of a fresh cycle.

Pacing & Formatting:

The copy is highly aggressive in its brevity. It relies heavily on visual hierarchy.

Structure: High-impact Title ➔ Date Range (Urgency) ➔ 2-sentence feature summary ➔ Immediate Call to Action (CTA).

There is zero long-form paragraph text. It is optimized for gamers who scan rather than read.

3. Messaging & Positioning Strategy

Brand Voice & Tone:

Authoritative, tactical, urgent, and direct.

The vocabulary leans into military and competitive phrasing: "neutralizador" (neutralizer), "escudos balísticos" (ballistic shields), "Divisão Lendária" (Legendary Division), "Prepare-se" (Get ready).

Pain Points & Objections (And How They Are Handled):

Objection 1: "This game is old, I missed the boat."

Solution: The Hero headline explicitly states a "New Era" has begun, wiping the slate clean and giving newcomers permission to join without feeling behind.

Objection 2: "I don't want to risk money on a hard game I might not like."

Solution: The massive "Acesso Gratuito" (Free Access) button is placed right next to the buy button. This is the ultimate risk-reversal tactic.

Objection 3: "Is this game a dead game/abandoned by devs?"

Solution: Prominent links to Patch Notes, ongoing Twitch drops, and a specific roadmap calendar (Sept 1 - Dec 1) prove active, heavily funded developer support.

4. Micro-Copy & Conversion Triggers

Frictionless Duality (The CTA Strategy): The page brilliantly utilizes dual CTAs. By placing "Acesso Gratuito" (Free Access) as a primary button, they capture the top-of-funnel leads. By placing "Membership / Passe de Batalha" immediately next to it, they simultaneously service their bottom-of-funnel whales (high-spending players).

Urgency & Scarcity Injectors:

Dates are used as weapons: "DE 1º DE SETEMBRO A 1º DE DEZEMBRO" (From Sept 1 to Dec 1). This time-boxes the value of the current Battle Pass, forcing the user to act now or lose out on exclusive items forever.

Psychological Trust Triggers:

Transparency: The button "Ver Notas da Atualização" (View Patch Notes) is a massive trust builder in the gaming community. It signals developer transparency and accountability.

Ecosystem Integration: Mentioning "Twitch Drops" borrows authority and social proof from Twitch, signaling to the user that millions of other people are actively watching and playing this game.

Micro-Commitments:

"Saiba mais" (Learn more) and "Leia mais" (Read more) are used on the community posts to keep users engaged on the domain without asking for an immediate credit card swipe.
5. FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) & Objection Routing

Observation on Structure: Unlike traditional SaaS or e-commerce landing pages, this page completely omits a standard accordion-style FAQ section.

Functional Goal: To eliminate visual clutter and keep the user hyper-focused on the primary conversion actions (Play Free, Buy, or Check the new Season). Instead of text-heavy answers, the page routes users to dedicated hubs or answers objections implicitly through UI design.

Implicit Questions Answered via UI/Copy:

Question: "Can I try this before spending money?"

CRO Solution: Answered instantly via the massive, persistent "Acesso Gratuito" (Free Access) button in the Hero section.

Question: "Is the game still active? Are the servers working?"

CRO Solution: Answered via the "Status do serviço" (Service Status) utility link and the active news feed.

Question: "What exactly is changing in the new season?"

CRO Solution: Answered via the "Ver Notas da Atualização" (View Patch Notes) button, which acts as a technical FAQ for hardcore players.

Question: "Where do I get help if my game crashes?"

CRO Solution: Pushed to the secondary navigation under "Serviços" ➔ "Suporte" / "Report a Bug".

CRO Takeaway & Blueprint Application: In the highly visual gaming industry, long-form FAQ accordions create unnecessary friction and break the immersive experience. The strategy here is "Show, Don't Tell." Major buying objections (price, active player base, game updates) are answered directly through hero buttons, dynamic news feeds, and utility navigation rather than a wall of text.
